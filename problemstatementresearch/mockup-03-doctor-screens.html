AI Briefing Card — Key UX Decisions The briefing card must load in <1 second — doctor is standing outside the consultation room. RAG query over the patient's vector store runs in the background when the appointment is confirmed (not when the doctor taps). The "Ask about Atorvastatin compliance" is a AI-generated prompt that helps doctors who forget follow-up questions. This alone reduces missed care opportunities.

💡 Preferred Path: Prescription Photo → Auto-Extract Vitals
1
Doctor writes prescription on paper as normal (BP: 124/82, Wt: 72kg, etc.)
2
Receptionist or patient photos the prescription → uploads to HealthBridge
3
Google Vision API + LLM extract: vitals, medicines, diagnosis, dosage → auto-populate patient record
Result: Doctor never touches the app. Patient gets a digital record of every paper prescription. Vitals trend is built automatically over time.
Why this is the right MVP choice Asking doctors to type vitals into an app during consultation adds friction and resistance. But they already write vitals on prescriptions. OCR extraction after the fact costs zero doctor effort and gives HealthBridge the same data. The "Quick Vitals Update" screen exists for forward-thinking doctors who go digital-first, but it's never the default flow. Zero doctor behavior change required for Phase 1.
