## =============================================
## PART E: KEY TAKEAWAYS FROM JOB DESCRIPTION ANALYSIS
## =============================================

1. **The 2026 AI Engineer is a Generalist**: Job descriptions are getting 
   longer and more demanding. Companies want people who can do ML, GenAI, 
   data engineering, deployment, and cloud — all in one role.

2. **GenAI is No Longer Optional**: 70%+ of roles mention Generative AI. 
   You cannot be a competitive AI professional without GenAI skills.

3. **Agentic AI is the Next Wave**: Multiple companies (Amazon, Persistent, 
   HCLTech, Goldman Sachs) now list Agentic AI as a requirement. 
   This will only grow.

4. **Production > Prototyping**: MLOps, Docker, CI/CD, Model Monitoring 
   appear in 30-50% of listings. Companies want AI in production, 
   not just Jupyter notebooks.

5. **Cloud Skills are Table Stakes**: 60%+ require at least one cloud 
   platform. Multi-cloud (2-3 platforms) is increasingly expected.

6. **Domain Knowledge Pays Premium**: AI + Finance, AI + Healthcare, 
   AI + Defense all command salary premiums of 15-40%.

7. **India Leads in Volume, US Leads in Salary**: India has 25,000+ 
   open AI roles (mass hiring), while US pays 3-5x more per role.

8. **BFSI is the Biggest AI Spender**: Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, 
   JPMorgan, BlackRock — all aggressively hiring AI talent in India.

9. **IT Services are the Gateway**: Accenture (3,789 roles), Infosys, 
   TCS are the easiest path into AI careers for most professionals.

10. **Remote Work is Rare in India**: 90%+ of Indian AI jobs are 
    office-based. Remote remains the exception, not the norm.
